Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel outside Mainpuri for destination weddings?

Yes. Ramanand Rajput and the crew regularly travel across Uttar Pradesh (Agra, Etawah, Shikohabad, Kanpur, Lucknow) and beyond for multi-day weddings.

When do we receive the finalized photobooks and raw videos?

Candid teasers are delivered within 7-10 days. Full 4K films and luxury velvet photobooks are designed, client-approved, and delivered within 3-4 weeks.

Can we customize our photography package?

Every celebration is unique. You can freely combine drone aerials, live streaming, additional traditional cameras, or parent miniature albums as needed.

How can I book an in-person studio visit?

You can visit us directly at Ishan Nadi Pull, near V-Bazaar in Gopal Nagar, Mainpuri, or simply call 8171969639 anytime between 9 AM and 9 PM.
